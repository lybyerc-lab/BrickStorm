# Content Pass 0.3.5

Foundation 0.3.5 turns the opening farm from a loose systems playground into a concentrated 60–90 second classic brick-adventure slice. The design reference is early Traveller's Tales grammar: bright readable interactables against a stylized real-world backdrop, frequent short payoffs, character reactions, sound, and world-changing builds.

## Authored first-minute chain

The opening path is stud trail → wrench → generator build → wrench repair → powered sensor cage → first sensor kit. A reactive second storm chaser points toward the next beat and celebrates the powered-gate payoff. The broader farm remains available afterward rather than becoming a corridor.

## Character presentation

The actor presentation owns explicit idle, start, run, pivot, air, and land poses. A first-step punch, squared hinge stride, planted reversal, airborne pose, landing squash, head/torso response, smash twist, and visible carried tools are separate from the gameplay motor.

## Original audio feedback

Original locally generated sounds cover silver/gold/blue/purple studs, smash impacts, build snaps, build completion, tool pickup, tool use, secret reveals, and looping storm wind. No licensed music, dialogue, or extracted commercial-game audio is included.

## HUD and camera grammar

The HUD is intentionally shallower than the previous prototype dashboard: objective, status/tools, stud progress, and storm pressure are compact. Canonical stud pickup values flash in their tier color. Important consequences can request short camera focus cues before normal framing returns.

## Environment contrast

The world now distinguishes background from interactable language. Stylized non-brick farmhouse/silo silhouettes, rounded tree lines, roadside grass, surrounding prairie, distant fields, storm sky, fog, and horizon forms create Oklahoma context. Brick-built vehicles, characters, tools, buildables, fences, and smashables remain the bright gameplay layer. The accepted continuous road/ground collision remains unchanged.
