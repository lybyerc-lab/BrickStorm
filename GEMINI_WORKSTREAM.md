# Gemini BrickStorm Workstream

This branch is owned by the Gemini / Antigravity implementation of **LEGO: Twister (BrickStorm)**.

## Creative Identity & Vision
- Target Experience: Classic Traveller's Tales era (2005-2008) licensed adaptation of the movie *Twister* (1996).
- Core Pillars:
  1. **Smashing & The Stud Economy**: Everything made of bricks shatters into physical pieces and canonical stud denominations (Silver 10, Gold 100, Blue 1000, Purple 10000) that vacuum toward the player with pitch-scaling audio chimes.
  2. **Bouncing Build Piles**: Interactive rebuildables that bounce and clatter rhythmically until assembled by holding the Build key.
  3. **The Twister Is a Dynamic Force**: A procedurally animated funnel that carves through Wakita farmsteads, peeling roof tiles, snapping fences, throwing airborne cows, and defining 4 concentric Risk Bands with stud score multipliers (Green 1x, Yellow 2x, Orange 3x, Red 5x).
  4. **The Dorothy Climax**: Recover and assemble the Dorothy sensor pack, push it into the direct path of the funnel, and release hundreds of glowing sensors into the vortex.
  5. **Authentic Minifigure Controller**: Dual-character pair (Jo and Bill) featuring classic toy waddling locomotion, punch combos, shoulder charging, 4 hearts, and comedic break-apart respawns (Law 2: No permadeath, only a stud toll).
  6. **Zero External Bloat**: Full procedural mesh and shader generation for crisp LEGO stud pitch, chamfers, and Oklahoma wheat/red dirt landscapes.
